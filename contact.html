Clear Things Up

Frequently Asked Questions

01. How far in advance should I place my order?

We recommend booking 2-4 weeks in advance for custom juice bars and smoothie stations. For large weddings and corporate events (100+ guests), please book 6-8 weeks ahead.

02. Do you offer tastings?

Yes! Tasting flights are available Monday-Thursday by appointment. A small fee applies, which is credited toward your final catering order.

03. What's your delivery radius?

We deliver within 50 miles of Los Angeles. Event setup and delivery is free within 30 miles. Travel fees apply for locations beyond.

04. Can you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Absolutely! All our bases are 100% vegan, and we offer gluten-free, dairy-free, and nut-free blending options. Please specify your needs when ordering.

05. What is your cancellation policy?

Full refund available up to 14 days before event. 50% refund between 7-13 days. No refunds within 7 days of event due to fresh ingredient sourcing.

06. Do you provide setup and cleanup?

Yes! All catering packages include professional delivery, styling, and setup. Post-event pickup and bar breakdown is included for Platinum packages.
